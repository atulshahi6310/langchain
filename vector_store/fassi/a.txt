**Ramayana – Short Story**

The **Ramayana** is one of the greatest ancient Indian epics. It tells the story of **Lord Rama**, the prince of Ayodhya, who is known for his courage, honesty, and devotion to duty.

Rama is sent into exile in the forest for fourteen years along with his wife, **Sita**, and his loyal brother, **Lakshmana**. During their exile, the demon king **Ravana** kidnaps Sita and takes her to his kingdom, Lanka.

To rescue Sita, Rama forms an alliance with the monkey king **Sugriva** and the devoted warrior **Hanuman**. Hanuman discovers Sita in Lanka and delivers Rama's message to her. Rama then leads an army to Lanka, where a great battle takes place. Ravana is defeated, and Sita is rescued.

After completing his exile, Rama, Sita, and Lakshmana return to Ayodhya, where Rama is crowned king. His rule, known as **Rama Rajya**, is remembered as a time of peace, justice, and prosperity.

The Ramayana teaches important values such as truth, duty, loyalty, courage, and the victory of good over evil.
